Wittgenstein's Fallacy

June 1, 2026

"If there were a verb meaning 'to believe falsely,' it would not have any significant first person, present indicative."

This is a dumb claim. Sure, people don't, consciously, go about believing false things on purpose. But there is no human being, and probably never has been, who does not believe falsely. I have false beliefs. I would try to stop believing them if I noticed them; I have done that in the past. Usually it works. ...I believe. But, to use the language of logic, there exists a statement p such that I believe p and p is false. There probably always will be; if I catch today's p, I'll still have tomorrow's. I habitually believe falsely, and so does everyone else. It's just annoying to caveat that constantly, because it's so obvious and vaguely shameful.
